# Writing · repaso de las Units 1–5

**Curso:** 2º DAW · Inglés Profesional GS  
**Tipo:** writing de práctica (B2, unas 340 palabras)  
**Tema:** un viaje de fin de semana que salió regular (tema neutro, sin tecnicismos)  
**Objetivo:** usar al menos una estructura de cada bloque de teoría: tiempos
verbales, condicionales, *wish clauses*, modales y modales perfectos

> Los números en volandas <span class="marca">1</span> remiten a la tabla del
> final. Cada número es una estructura distinta de la teoría; ninguna está puesta
> de relleno.

## El texto

<div class="texto">

**A weekend I will not forget**

I <b>live</b><span class="marca">1</span> in Algeciras, a city in the south of
Spain, and I love travelling by train. Since January I <b>have been planning</b><span class="marca">2</span>
a short trip to Lisbon with my friend Marta. This month I <b>have already
booked</b><span class="marca">3</span> the hotel and the tickets, so everything
seemed ready.

Two weeks ago we <b>travelled</b><span class="marca">4</span> to Lisbon for the
weekend. While we <b>were waiting</b><span class="marca">5</span> at the station,
we realised that we <b>had left</b><span class="marca">6</span> the tickets at
home. We <b>had been queuing</b><span class="marca">7</span> for half an hour, so
we were tired and nervous, and the train left without us.

<b>If we had printed</b> the tickets, <b>we would have caught</b><span class="marca">8</span>
the first train. <b>If I had checked</b> the folder before leaving, we <b>would
be</b><span class="marca">9</span> in Lisbon now. <b>If I were</b> more careful,
<b>I would keep</b><span class="marca">10</span> everything in one bag. In
Portugal, <b>if you buy</b> a ticket online, you <b>get</b><span class="marca">11</span>
a small discount. Next year I <b>am going to save</b><span class="marca">12</span>
the tickets on my phone, and <b>if we travel</b> again, <b>we will leave</b><span class="marca">13</span>
home earlier.

<b>Unless</b><span class="marca">14</span> we find a cheap flight, we will go by
bus, so I always keep some cash <b>in case</b><span class="marca">15</span> the
card stops working. <b>As long as</b><span class="marca">16</span> we book early,
the trip will be cheap. At the moment I <b>am looking</b><span class="marca">17</span>
at prices, and by Friday we <b>will have decided</b><span class="marca">18</span>
the dates. Next Saturday at eight we <b>will be travelling</b><span class="marca">19</span>
to Seville to visit my family.

I <b>wish I had printed</b><span class="marca">20</span> the tickets that
morning. I also <b>wish the station were</b><span class="marca">21</span> closer
to my house, I <b>wish my friend would arrive</b><span class="marca">22</span> on
time, and I <b>wish I could</b><span class="marca">23</span> travel without
thinking about money.

The lesson is simple. You <b>must</b><span class="marca">24</span> arrive early
at the station, but you <b>don't have to</b><span class="marca">25</span> pay for
a taxi: the bus is fine. You <b>should</b><span class="marca">26</span> check your
bag before you leave, and you <b>needn't</b><span class="marca">27</span> print
every document. The tickets <b>must have stayed</b><span class="marca">28</span>
in the kitchen, and we <b>should have checked</b><span class="marca">29</span> the
folder first. We <b>might have lost</b><span class="marca">30</span> the money,
and we <b>needn't have run</b><span class="marca">31</span> to the platform: the
train was late anyway. Now I <b>can</b><span class="marca">32</span> laugh about
it, but that morning I wanted to go home.

</div>

## Qué se ha usado y por qué

| # | Estructura | Unidad | Por qué esa y no otra |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Present simple · *I live* | 1 | Situación estable, sin fecha de fin. |
| 2 | Present perfect continuous · *have been planning* | 1 | Empezó en enero y sigue: duración hasta ahora. |
| 3 | Present perfect simple · *have already booked* | 1 | Hecho reciente con resultado presente (*this month* sigue abierto). |
| 4 | Past simple · *we travelled* | 1 | Momento concreto y cerrado: *two weeks ago*. |
| 5 | Past continuous · *we were waiting* | 1 | Acción en desarrollo en ese momento. |
| 6 | Past perfect · *we had left* | 1 | Anterior a otra acción pasada (dejar las entradas antes de llegar). |
| 7 | Past perfect continuous · *had been queuing* | 1 | Duración de algo anterior al problema: media hora de cola. |
| 8 | Third conditional · *If we had printed…, we would have caught* | 2 | Pasado que no ocurrió y su consecuencia evitada. |
| 9 | Mixed conditional · *If I had checked…, we would be* | 2 | Condición pasada con resultado en el presente (*now*). |
| 10 | Second conditional · *If I were…, I would keep* | 2 | Hipótesis sobre el presente; con *be* se usa *were*. |
| 11 | Zero conditional · *if you buy…, you get* | 2 | Verdad general, siempre igual. |
| 12 | *Be going to* · *am going to save* | 1 | Decisión ya tomada. |
| 13 | First conditional · *if we travel…, we will leave* | 2 | Posibilidad real en el futuro. |
| 14 | *Unless* | 2 | Equivale a *if … not*: si no encontramos vuelo. |
| 15 | *In case* | 2 | Precaución previa, no condición. |
| 16 | *As long as* | 2 | Condición necesaria: con tal de que. |
| 17 | Present continuous · *am looking* | 1 | Acción en curso estos días. |
| 18 | Future perfect · *will have decided* | 1 | Terminado antes de una fecha (*by Friday*). |
| 19 | Future continuous · *will be travelling* | 1 | En desarrollo en un momento futuro concreto. |
| 20 | Wish + past perfect · *wish I had printed* | 3 | Arrepentimiento: ya no se puede cambiar. |
| 21 | Wish + past simple · *wish the station were* | 3 | Deseo sobre el presente; *were* en hipótesis. |
| 22 | Wish + would · *wish she would arrive* | 3 | Queja por una conducta repetida de otro. |
| 23 | Wish + could · *wish I could travel* | 3 | Capacidad que no se tiene. |
| 24 | *Must* · obligación | 5 | Obligación fuerte, la plantea quien habla. |
| 25 | *Don't have to* | 5 | **No** hay obligación (no es prohibición). |
| 26 | *Should* | 5 | Consejo. |
| 27 | *Needn't* | 5 | Ausencia de necesidad, igual que *don't have to*. |
| 28 | *Must have* + participio | 5 | Deducción lógica sobre el pasado. |
| 29 | *Should have* + participio | 5 | Reproche: no se hizo lo que tocaba. |
| 30 | *Might have* + participio | 5 | Posibilidad en el pasado, sin certeza. |
| 31 | *Needn't have* + participio | 5 | Se hizo algo que no hacía falta. |
| 32 | *Can* · habilidad | 5 | Capacidad actual. |

<div class="nota">
Las tres trampas que están puestas a propósito: <b>must</b> frente a
<b>don't have to</b> (obligación frente a ausencia), <b>needn't have run</b>
frente a <b>didn't have to run</b> (se hizo sin necesidad frente a no era
necesario), y <b>in case</b> frente a <b>if</b> (precaución frente a condición).
</div>

## Unidad 4 · Rephrasing sobre el mismo texto

El rephrasing no se escribe «dentro» de un texto: se practica transformando sus
frases. Estas seis salen del writing.

| Frase original | Transformada |
|---|---|
| We didn't print the tickets, so we missed the first train. | **If we had printed** the tickets, we **would have caught** the first train. |
| I didn't check the folder, and now we are not in Lisbon. | **If I had checked** the folder, we **would be** in Lisbon now. |
| It wasn't necessary to run to the platform. | We **needn't have run** to the platform. |
| I'm sure the tickets stayed in the kitchen. | The tickets **must have stayed** in the kitchen. |
| My friend is always late. | I **wish** my friend **would arrive** on time. |
| We won't go by train if we don't find cheap tickets. | **Unless** we find cheap tickets, we **won't go** by train. |

<div class="nota">
Regla del ejercicio: si te dan la palabra de arranque (<b>Unless</b>,
<b>If</b>, <b>I wish</b>, <b>needn't</b>…), esa palabra tiene que aparecer y la
frase no puede cambiar de significado. Si cambias el tiempo verbal pero el
sentido se mueve, la respuesta está mal aunque la gramática sea correcta.
</div>

## Cómo reutilizarlo

- **Para un writing libre:** la estructura del texto sirve tal cual — presente
  para el contexto, pasado para la anécdota, condicionales y *wish* para el
  análisis, modales para la conclusión. Solo hay que cambiar el tema.
- **Para el speaking:** recorta los dos primeros párrafos y la conclusión; con eso
  tienes 90 segundos y sigues usando ocho o nueve estructuras distintas.
- **Para repasar:** tapa la columna «Por qué» de la tabla y trata de explicar tú
  cada número. Si fallas en el 9, el 21 o el 31, vuelve a *Mixed conditionals*,
  a *wish + past simple* y a los modales perfectos: son los tres puntos donde
  más se cae.

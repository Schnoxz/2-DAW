## 1. Present Perfect Simple + Present Continuous

> My family **have bought** (buy) some land in southern France recently. They **are building** (build) a summer house there at the moment.

- **have bought** → *recently* es una marca del **Present Perfect Simple** (pasado reciente relevante ahora).
- **are building** → *at the moment* (now) es la marca del **Present Continuous**.

## 2. Present Continuous (arrangement) + Present Perfect Continuous

> Andy and Mary **are going** (go) to a concert tomorrow night. They **have been looking forward to** (look forward to) it the whole week.

- **are going** → planes con expresión de tiempo futuro (*tomorrow night*): **Present Continuous** para hablar de future arrangements (planes).
- **have been looking forward to** → *the whole week* (duración) = **Present Perfect Continuous**.

## 3. Present Simple (hábito) + Present Simple (verdad general)

> Jonathon **watches** (watch) the news on TV every day and it **helps** (help) him with his English.

- **watches** → *every day* = hábito → **Present Simple**, 3ª persona singular con **-es**.
- **helps** → efecto permanente/verdad general → **Present Simple**.

## 4. Narración en pasado + condicional

> My car **broke** (break) down when I **was driving** (drive) home from work. I **would fix** (fix) it if I **knew** (know) what was wrong. But I didn't so **had** (have) to take it to the garage.

- **broke** → acción puntual pasada → **Past Simple** (verbo irregular *break → broke*).
- **was driving** → escena en curso cuando ocurrió algo más corto (*when*) → **Past Continuous**.
- **would fix / knew** → hipótesis irreal sobre el pasado → **Segundo condicional** (*would + base / Past Simple*).
- **had to** → *have to* en pasado → **Past Simple** (*had to*).

> ⚠️ **Variante aceptada:** si se interpreta como un hecho real y posible, valdría el **primer condicional**: *I **will fix** it if I **know** what was wrong*. La versión con *would/knew* encaja mejor porque el texto continúa con *But I didn't so I had to…* (el condicional no se cumplió).

## 5. Past Simple + Past Perfect Simple

> When he **found** (find) Microsoft, Bill Gates was only 20 years old. He **had already written** (write) his first computer programme six years earlier.

- **found** → **Past Simple**.
- **had already written** → *six years earlier* + *already* → un pasado más lejano → **Past Perfect Simple** (*already* con *had*).

## 6. Narración en pasado (mixto)

> An accident **happened** (happen) near my house last night. A car **hit** (hit) a young man. He **was riding** (ride) his bike when someone in front of him suddenly **opened** (open) a car door. Many people **saw** (see) the accident. The police **interrogated** (interrogate) them last night.

- **happened / hit / opened / saw / interrogated** → **Past Simple** (hechos completados; *last night*).
- **was riding** → acción larga en curso interrumpida por otra más corta (*when someone… opened*) → **Past Continuous**.

## 7. Reported speech (futuro en el pasado)

> Mrs Smith said that one day she **would retire** (retire) from teaching. She said that she **would spend** (spend) her new free time learning about computers.

- **said** está en pasado, así que el futuro del original pasa a **would + base form**.
- Los dos verbos convergen al mismo tiempo: **would retire / would spend**.

## 8. Past Simple + Past Continuous

> I **didn't sleep** (not sleep) at all last night. Someone **was listening** (listen) to music all night.

- **didn't sleep** → **Past Simple negativo** con *didn't*.
- **was listening** → *all night* = duración en pasado → **Past Continuous**.

## 9. Past Simple, Past Perfect y condicional

> I **saw** (see) a film a week ago, but I **didn't enjoy** (not enjoy) it very much because I **had already read** (read) the book. If I **hadn't read** (not read) the book I **would probably enjoy** (enjoy) the film more.

- **saw / didn't enjoy** → **Past Simple** (*a week ago*).
- **had already read** → la lectura es anterior a la visión de la película → **Past Perfect Simple**.
- **hadn't read / would probably enjoy** → **segundo condicional** (hipótesis irreal en el pasado: *If + Past Perfect, would + base*).

## 10. Past Perfect Simple

> The judge sentenced the man to eight years in prison because he **had robbed** (rob) a bank.

- El robo es **anterior** al juicio → **Past Perfect Simple** (*had + participio*).
- Cuidado: aquí no puede ser *Past Simple*, porque el especiais de la chronologie.

## 11. Past Perfect Continuous + Past Simple

> They **had been standing** (stand) in the queue for over an hour when the manager **told** (tell) them that there were no more tickets.

- **had been standing** → *for over an hour* = duración, y además la acción aún no había terminado cuando llegó el dato nuevo → **Past Perfect Continuous**.
- **told** → acción puntual e instantánea que corta la anterior (*when*) → **Past Simple**.

## 12. Past Simple + Past Perfect Simple

> Alan **was** (be) in the car accident yesterday. The other driver **lost** (lose) control of his car because he **had fallen** (fall) asleep.

- **was** → **Past Simple** del verbo *be* (*yesterday*).
- **lost** → **Past Simple** (verbo irregular).
- **had fallen** → haberse dormido **antes** de perder el control → **Past Perfect Simple**.

## 13. Present Perfect Simple + Past Simple

> She **hasn't seen** (not see) her father since he **started** (start) to work in Marseille two years ago.

- **hasn't seen** → *since* = estado que empieza en el pasado y continúa → **Present Perfect Simple**.
- **started** → momento concreto y delimitado del pasado (*two years ago*) → **Past Simple**.

## 14. Past Continuous

> I **was sleeping** (sleep) when the fire broke out.

- Patrón clásico **Past Continuous + Past Simple**: *was sleeping* (escena) + *broke out* (evento).

## 15. Reported speech en pasado

> Linda phoned and explained that she **couldn't** (not can) to come to the party the next day because she **was** (be) still sick.

- **couldn't** → *can* en pasado → **Past Simple** de *can*.
- **was** → **Past Simple** de *be*; *still* refuerza la idea de continuidad.
- 💡 En un examen de *reported speech* también se acepta **wouldn't be able to** (*can* → *ability* en pasado).

## 16. Diálogo en Present Perfect Simple

> I **have just seen** (just see) the film "The Da Vinci Code". – **Have** (you see) **seen** it too? – No, I **haven't seen** (not have) it but I **have read** (read) the book.

- **just / ever / before / twice** → marcas del **Present Perfect Simple**; en preguntas y negativos se necesita el auxiliar **have/has** delante del participio.
- *I have just seen* / *Have you seen?* / *I haven't seen (it)* / *I have read*.

## 17. Present Continuous ×3 (planes y Disposable)

> My sister **is flying** (fly) home from London today. Her flight **is arriving** (arrive) in an hour so I **am leaving** (leave) for the airport right now to get there in time.

- Las tres son **Present Continuous**:
  - **is flying** → plan programado (*today*).
  - **is arriving** → horario previsto (*in an hour*).
  - **am leaving** → decisión/propósito inmediato (*right now*).
- 💡 En el último hueco también se acepta **must leave** (*debo irme*): *must* en presente con intención firme.

## 18. Present Simple en el primer condicional

> Unless he **sells** (sell) more he won't get much money.

- *Unless* (= *if... not*) introduce una **cláusula de tiempo futuro**, por lo que se usa **Present Simple**.
- *won't get* = *will not* (forma del *will* + base) — el *will* **no** cambia en la cláusula condicional.

## 19. Pasado continuo + pasado perfecto

> While he **was waiting** (wait) for the bus there **was** (be) a robbery at the bank. After the robbers **had gone** (go) away the police **came** (come) but they **couldn't** (not can) to catch them.

- **was waiting** → *while* + acción en curso → **Past Continuous**.
- **was** → otro hecho en curso al mismo tiempo → **Past Continuous** (*be* irregular: *was/were*).
- **had gone** → el atraco ya había ocurrido cuando llegaron los policías (*after*) → **Past Perfect Simple**.
- **came** → **Past Simple**.
- **couldn't** → **Past Simple** de *can*.

## 20. Used to + Past Simple + Present Perfect Simple

> I **used to ski** (use to ski) when I was at the university but I **broke** (break) a leg five years ago and since then I **haven't skied** (not ski) any more.

- **used to ski** → hábito del pasado ya terminado → **used to + base form**.
- **broke** → **Past Simple** (*five years ago*).
- **haven't skied** → *since then* = desde ese momento hasta hoy, sin volver a hacerlo → **Present Perfect Simple**.

## Trampas del ejercicio (para repasar)

1. **Orden de la narración en pasado:** el hecho que *ocurre* va en **Past Simple** y lo que estaba pasando, en **Past Continuous** (*He was riding… when someone opened…*).
2. **Más tarde = más perfecto:** cuando un pasado contiene otro pasado, el más antiguo va a **Past Perfect** (*he had written*, *he had fallen asleep*).
3. **Condicionales con verbos irregulares:** en el 2º condicional la forma es *would + base*, nunca *would + participio*: *would fix*, *would retire*, *would probably enjoy*.
4. **Stative verbs:** *know, be, love, like, have (posesión)* no van en continuo.
5. **La cláusula *if / unless / when* no lleva *will*:** si el ejercicio pide el verbo de la parte condicional, es **Present Simple** aunque el resultado sea futuro.

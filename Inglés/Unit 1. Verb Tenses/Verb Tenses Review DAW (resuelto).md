# Verb Tenses Review DAW — Resuelto

## Tense Review #1 — What tense is it? Underline the structure. What is the use/rule?

**1. I often start work at 8:30 every day.**
- Tense: Present Simple
- Structure: infinitive (+ -s 3ª pers.)
- Use / Rule: Hábito / rutina

**2. The world is round.**
- Tense: Present Simple
- Structure: infinitive / be
- Use / Rule: Hecho o algo verdadero

**3. I was watching TV last night.**
- Tense: Past Continuous
- Structure: was/were + -ing
- Use / Rule: Actividad en el pasado (en desarrollo)

**4. I was watching TV last night when she called me.**
- Tense: Past Continuous + Past Simple
- Structure: was/were + -ing / past simple
- Use / Rule: Actividad en desarrollo interrumpida por otra acción

**5. We're doing a grammar review.**
- Tense: Present Continuous
- Structure: am/is/are + -ing
- Use / Rule: Actividad que ocurre ahora

**6. I'm having my haircut today at 3:00.**
- Tense: Present Continuous
- Structure: am/is/are + -ing
- Use / Rule: Futuro acordado / concertado

**7. I will be travelling to London tomorrow evening.**
- Tense: Future Continuous
- Structure: will + be + -ing
- Use / Rule: Actividad en el futuro

**8. I lived in Spain in 2009.**
- Tense: Past Simple
- Structure: past simple form
- Use / Rule: Acción terminada en el pasado

**9. They've lived in Spain since 2011.**
- Tense: Present Perfect Simple
- Structure: have/has + past participle
- Use / Rule: Acción pasada conectada con el presente (since)

**10. I've been living in Spain for 9 years.**
- Tense: Present Perfect Continuous
- Structure: have/has + been + -ing
- Use / Rule: Actividad que empezó en el pasado y continúa hasta ahora

**11. I've never been to Spain.**
- Tense: Present Perfect Simple
- Structure: have/has + past participle
- Use / Rule: Experiencia pasada

**12. I think I'll go shopping this afternoon.**
- Tense: Future Simple
- Structure: will + infinitive
- Use / Rule: Decisión espontánea / predicción

**13. I'm going shopping this afternoon.**
- Tense: Future (going to)
- Structure: going to + infinitive
- Use / Rule: Plan / decisión tomada antes de hablar

**14. John was not at home; he had gone shopping.**
- Tense: Past Perfect Simple
- Structure: had + past participle
- Use / Rule: El "pasado del pasado" (before/after/when)

**15. John had been shopping for 2 hours before he got home.**
- Tense: Past Perfect Continuous
- Structure: had + been + -ing
- Use / Rule: Actividad pasada anterior a otra acción pasada

**16. The world cup will be held in Russia in 2018.**
- Tense: Future Simple (passive)
- Structure: will + be + past participle
- Use / Rule: Hecho futuro / predicción

**17. We will live on Mars in 2050.**
- Tense: Future Simple
- Structure: will + infinitive
- Use / Rule: Hecho futuro / predicción

**18. I'll help you with your homework later.**
- Tense: Future Simple
- Structure: will + infinitive
- Use / Rule: Ofrecimiento

**19. The train leaves at 3:45.**
- Tense: Present Simple
- Structure: infinitive (+ -s)
- Use / Rule: Horario / programación

**20. Look at those black clouds — It's going to rain.**
- Tense: Future (going to)
- Structure: going to + infinitive
- Use / Rule: Predicción basada en evidencia presente

**21. By June, I'll have been here for 2 months.**
- Tense: Future Perfect Simple
- Structure: will + have + past participle
- Use / Rule: Acción completada para un momento del futuro

**22. By June, I'll have been living here for 2 months.**
- Tense: Future Perfect Continuous
- Structure: will + have + been + -ing
- Use / Rule: Actividad completada para un momento del futuro

## Notes / Alternatives

- 1) También válido *has been improving* (Present Perfect Continuous); "every day" expresa un cambio gradual, de ahí el continuo.
- 8) "Of course I will be late" es irónico (él es quien siempre llega tarde, como reproduce la frase siguiente).
- 11) *to catch* = infinitivo de finalidad ("para coger").
- 14) Negativo por el "until": *there wasn't another train until 8:45*.
- 15) *were* = subjuntivo en 2ª condicional (*If I were you*).
- 17) Tras *before* referido al futuro se usa Present Simple (*sets sail*).
- 20) También posible *closed*; *was closing* resalta la acción en desarrollo.
- 26) Pregunta en Past Simple: *Did you hear?*
- 28) *If only + Past Perfect* expresa arrepentimiento (3ª condicional).

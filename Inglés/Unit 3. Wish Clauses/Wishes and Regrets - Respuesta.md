## A. Present or past?

| | Frase | Respuesta |
|---|---|---|
| a. | I wish I was taller. I'd love to be a supermodel. | **present** |
| b. | I wish I had eaten breakfast. I'm absolutely starving. | **past** |
| c. | If only it would stop raining. I want to go outside. | **present** |
| d. | The bus still hasn't arrived. If only we had driven. | **past** |
| e. | I wish I was an only child. My sister is so annoying. | **present** |

## B. Circle the correct tense

1. If only I didn't eat / **hadn't eaten** so much cake at the party. I feel quite ill now.
2. I wish I **lived** / had lived in the countryside. I'm fed up with city life.
3. I wish I didn't shave / **hadn't shaved** off all my hair. My head is cold.
4. If only our lessons **were** / had been shorter. They make me sleepy!
5. I wish he stopped / **would stop** coughing. It's really irritating.

## C. Same meaning

1. I would like to live alone. → I wish **I lived alone**.
2. I hate my name. I want a different one. → If only **I had a different name**.
3. I wasn't careful enough. I regret it. → I wish **I had been more careful**.
4. She bites her nails. It's annoying. → I wish **she wouldn't bite her nails**.
5. Jen lied. Now she regrets it. → If only **she hadn't lied**.

## D. Verbs in the box

1. Peter wishes his wife **were** a better cook. She can't even make an omelette.
2. He's driving too fast. I wish he **would slow down**.
3. I wish I **hadn't come** by car. The roads are icy tonight, and parking was expensive.
4. Sally wishes she **had** a garden. She'd love to grow her own vegetables.
5. I wish I **hadn't drunk** so much coffee today. I'll never get to sleep.
6. I wish he **would stop** calling me. I don't want to speak to him.
7. If only I **understood** Japanese. If I did, I'd know what they were talking about.
8. We wish our parents **weren't** so strict. They don't let us do anything.
9. If only the bar **stayed** open later. I don't think I'll make it before closing time.
10. I wish I **had brought** an umbrella with me. It's pouring!

## E. My own endings

1. A superpower you would like to have. → I wish **I could teleport anywhere**.
2. A possession you would like to own. → I wish **I owned a small camper van**.
3. Something you regret doing as a child. → I wish **I hadn't given up the guitar**.
4. Something you wish a family member wouldn't do. → I wish **my brother wouldn't shout**.
5. A country you would like to live in. → I wish **I lived in Norway**.
6. Something you wish you had done in the past. → I wish **I had studied more English**.
7. A regret about a purchase you made. → I wish **I hadn't bought this phone**.
8. A skill you would like to have. → I wish **I could speak Japanese**.
9. Something you wish was different about your home. → I wish **my flat had a terrace**.
10. Something about yourself you would like to change. → I wish **I were more patient**.
